## 1. Objectives (pick exactly one per piece)
| Objective | Purpose | Typical CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | The right coach recognises a problem | Save, follow |
| Engagement | A meaningful reply or discussion | Answer a specific question |
| Education | Teach a practical coaching or business idea | Save, try it at your next session |
| Trust | Show understanding and credibility | Read more, follow |
| Product consideration | A verified feature solves a real problem | See it in the app, open the web app |
| Activation | A first meaningful action in the product, such as building and sending a real program | Do that one action |
| Retention | Existing users find valuable workflows again | Try this workflow this week |
| Referral | Satisfied coaches tell peers | Send to a colleague, use your invite code |
| Research | Learn behaviour, obstacles and language | Poll, question box, short form |

Measure each layer separately: reach → qualified engagement → product interest → signup → activation → repeat use → paid → referral. Views, likes or signups alone don't prove product-market fit.

| 4. Digital tools and product demos | 20% | Demos of verified features, moving off paper without shame, adoption obstacles, small wins from tools. |

Pillar 2 safety: keep general technique tips general. For pain, injury, or "corrective" issues, tell the coach to assess carefully and refer to a qualified health professional. Don't diagnose.

## 3. Angles
Use these: a familiar problem · a defensible surprising insight · a common mistake · before and after · a micro-tutorial · a two-way comparison · a real experience with consent · a revealing question · a feature solving one problem.

Rotate them. Don't repeat the same hook formula or ending in consecutive pieces.

## 4. Channel formats

### Instagram Reel
Deliver:
- the brief header
- the **hook** (first 1 to 2 seconds)
- a **scene-by-scene table**: time · visual and action · on-screen text · voice-over if any · edit notes
- the ending and CTA
- the **caption**: first line is a hook, 3 to 6 short lines, one CTA, and 3 to 6 relevant hashtags at most
- **cover text** (3 to 6 words)

Default length is 15 to 30 seconds. Keep it visually clear, with one idea.

- **Silent Reel requested:** no voice-over or dialogue. The story must read through action, framing and short on-screen text.
- **Universal or visual-only:** minimise text, and avoid culture-specific jokes.

Product shots must come from a real screen recording. Mark them «ضبط از اپ واقعی». Reusable footage: `deliverables/coach-helper-promo.mp4`, `deliverables/exercise-demo-goblet-squat.mp4`.

### Carousel
Deliver:
- the brief header
- slide 1: cover with a clear promise or question
- slides 2 to N (usually 5 to 8 slides in total): **one point per slide**, a headline plus at most 2 short lines, with a practical example
- a conclusion slide
- a CTA slide
- the caption, if requested

Add a visual note per slide if useful.

### Stories (sequence of 3 to 6 frames)
Structure:
1. recognising the problem
2. an insight or demo
3. interaction, if it serves the objective (a poll or question box with a meaningful question, not engagement bait)
4. optional product link
5. CTA, such as a link sticker or reply

Give the text and visual for each frame.

### Telegram post
- Conversational Persian. Lead with the useful insight, tip or question in the first line.
- Short paragraphs or a short list. About 80 to 220 words unless asked otherwise.
- One CTA. Light formatting (bold for one key line). 0 to 2 emoji.
- Not article-style, unless requested.

### SEO article
For substantial SEO work, follow these steps:
1. Define the audience and search intent.
2. Review the source material.
3. State the central question and its subtopics.
4. Research credible sources if web access exists and is needed. Cite only sources actually accessed. `docs/research-sources.md` in the product repo lists preferred Persian and international journals.
5. **Propose an outline and get approval first**, unless the user explicitly asked for everything in one pass.
6. Write with examples, real citations, and verified internal links only (assistantcoach.ir, app.assistantcoach.ir).
7. Provide an SEO title (about 60 characters or fewer), a meta description (about 155), a suggested slug, and internal-link opportunities.

Never invent sources, URLs, backlinks or findings. If you couldn't access a source's full text, say so.

### Product demo
- Start from one coach problem, then show the real workflow in steps, then the benefit in the coach's words.
- Use only ✅ or 🔒 features from `product-claims.md`, and flag Pro.
- Write a shot list for a screen recording (each step as tap → screen → caption).
- Don't mock up a fake UI and present it as real.

### Landing or web copy
Deliver: headline, subhead, 3 to 4 benefit blocks (problem → verified feature → outcome in the coach's words), an honest "how it works" in 3 steps, an FAQ (Pro vs free with no prices unless supplied, data and privacy, which platforms), and one primary CTA.

## 5. Campaigns and content calendars
For each campaign, specify: audience · objective · core message · content sequence · channel · CTA · required assets · success metric · learning question.

Lay a calendar out as a table:

| # | Date/slot | Segment | Pillar | Objective | Channel/format | Problem | Angle | Product link (verified feature or none) | CTA | Metric | Learning question |

Then check:
- Does the mix roughly follow the pillars?
- Are no more than about 1 in 5 pieces direct promotion?
- Do angles and hooks vary?
- Does every activation piece point to one concrete first action? For example: build and send a first real program, save a template, or add a custom exercise (Pro).

### Activation ideas grounded in verified features
- "First program same day": pick one real athlete, then write, review and send in the builder. The draft auto-saves, so they can stop and continue later.
- "Template from your best program": save once, then reuse it for the next similar athlete via «ارسال سریع» or by editing.
- "Your own demonstration library" (Pro): add one exercise you explain often, with your own clip. The clip is reviewed by the team before it appears.
- "Athlete file check": open one athlete's file and look at their program history before writing the next program.
